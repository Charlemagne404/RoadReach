Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 26, 2026

RoadReach provides map-based reachability visualizations for walking, cycling, and driving. This policy describes the limited information processed when you use the application.

Information processed

RoadReach processes location searches, selected map coordinates, travel mode choices, and distance settings so it can calculate and display reachability results.

Saved scenarios and recent locations may be stored in your browser local storage on your own device. This data is not required to leave your device to use the interface.

Third-party services

Reachability, routing, and geocoding requests may be sent to GraphHopper or other mapping providers configured by the operator of this deployment. Basemap tiles and font assets may also be requested from third-party providers as part of normal page rendering.

How data is used

Data is used only to fulfill map searches, generate reachability calculations, render map tiles, and maintain core application behavior.

Retention

Server-side retention depends on the infrastructure and logging policy of the deployment operator. Browser local storage remains until you clear it or remove saved scenarios.

Contact

RoadReach is made by Continental. For project contact, use contact.continental-hub.com.
